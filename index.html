02 / HOW IT WORKS

Keep the uncertainty connected.

Frozen protocol ↗
01

Use the past that was available

At 10:00 on D−1, take the latest 28 complete error days whose prices passed the 24-hour research delay. The July warm-up precedes the August–September replay.

02

Preserve each day’s pattern

Add each whole historical error vector to the target forecast. Correlated errors stay together. Twenty-eight paths remain 28 paths; no artificial sample inflation.

03

Optimize, then check reality

Minimize mean scenario cost under a tail budget. Freeze the schedule, settle at actual prices, and compare all policies without choosing a winner after the fact.

Assumptions and limits of this research

The load can run at any fraction of 1 MW in any quarter, consuming exactly 4 MWh per day. Real processes may require continuous operation, ramp limits or minimum runs. Those constraints, grid tariffs, taxes, imbalance costs and market frictions are omitted.

Forecasts are reused from Nordcast: ridge for DK1 and boosted median for DK2. This is familiar retrospective data, not a new independent forecast validation. The source can contain revised prices. The early commitment is hypothetical; after day-ahead prices are published a scheduler could use them directly.

Historical residuals may span different monthly model updates. Only 28 equally weighted paths inform each decision. Optimization uses the same paths as the reported risk estimate, so optimistic estimates and regime failures remain possible. This sample has 96 quarters every day; incompatible daylight-saving day lengths are rejected, not silently interpolated.
